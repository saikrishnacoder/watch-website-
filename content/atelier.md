## Design

Proportions are drawn by hand before they ever meet CAD. The 10:10 pose, the lume plot, the negative space of a dial — all decided here.

## Movement

Plates are beveled, wheels are circular-grained, and every jewel is seated by eye. Regulation happens in five positions over fourteen days.

## Case and crystal

Steel, gold or DLC is machined, brushed, and polished in alternating planes. The sapphire is double-domed so the dial seems to float.

## Assembly

A single watchmaker owns a piece from casing to final timing. Their punch mark sits inside the caseback. Ours, and theirs.
